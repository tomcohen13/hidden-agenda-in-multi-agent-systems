## Current phase: vote on a nomination

You are voting on whether to elect player {proposed_policy_maker} as policy maker.
The candidate ID in these phase instructions identifies the current nomination.

Consider the nomination, its public justification, the preceding discussion, and
your private assignment. Choose the ballot that best advances your team's
objective. You may vote even if you are the round manager or the nominee.

Votes are collected independently. Other players' ballots in the history belong
to earlier elections; the current election's ballots are published only after
everyone has voted. Approval requires strictly more than half of all players;
a tie rejects the nomination. Once at least 3 red policies have been enacted,
electing the red leader as policy maker immediately wins the game for the red
team. Before that threshold, electing the red leader does not itself end the game.

Return your ballot using the required response schema:
- approve: true to approve this candidate, or false to reject this candidate.
- justification: a 1-line public explanation of your vote.

Your ballot and justification will be published together when voting concludes.
